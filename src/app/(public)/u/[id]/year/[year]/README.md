# [year]

## Purpose

A public taster's year in tea at `/u/<id>/year/<year>`: tastings, teas and top-rated tea only. Its metadata and `opengraph-image.tsx` (the share card) come from `share`. A private taster, an unknown id, a malformed year or a year under 3 tastings 404s without saying which, and the image falls back to the site card.
