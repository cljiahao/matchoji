# [drink]

## Purpose

One drink: café link, base, style, serving and price, then the taster score and community score side by side, "Tea used here" linking to the tea page when set, the trusted-taster reviews, then the community reviews, then similar drinks in the same city. With no reviews it says so. A merged drink redirects to its survivor and a rejected drink to its café, permanently; an unknown or malformed slug is `notFound()`. Taster reviews never mix into the community list.
